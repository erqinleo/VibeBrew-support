Smart Suggestions

Adjust parameters based on bean state

同样的豆子，在刚烘焙时和放置一个月后，冲煮方法应该不同。VibeBrew 根据豆子新鲜度给出针对性的建议。

The same beans require different brewing approaches when fresh versus after a month. VibeBrew provides targeted suggestions based on bean freshness.

Suggestion Examples

新豆建议：延长闷蒸时间，让气体充分释放

Fresh beans: Extend bloom time for full gas release

Aged beans: Grind finer, increase water to compensate
